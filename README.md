# EduPulse AI
Intelligent Multi-Tenant Compliance & Audit Assistant
EduPulse AI is an intelligent compliance and internal audit platform designed to simplify regulatory management for organizations in highly regulated industries, including Fintech, Healthcare, and Higher Education.

The platform enables organizations to centralize regulatory documents, internal policies, and audit reports in one searchable knowledge base. Using advanced Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), users can interact with documents through natural language, verify compliance requirements, identify policy gaps, and generate audit-ready reports with references to original sources.
